  • Choose at the top whether the match is played to 31 or 41 points. Hands follow one another automatically until a team reaches the target.
  • You and North are a team. West and East are the other team.
  • Online: one player creates a table and shares the code; friends join with it. Empty seats are played by the computer, and a friend who leaves is replaced by the computer.
  • You deal first, then the deal passes to the player on your right, and so on around the table. Bidding and play also go to the right, starting with the player right of the dealer. If everyone else passes, the dealer is automatically bid 7 and may also choose a higher bid.
  • Everyone gets 5 cards first. Each player then bids once, from 7 to 13 tricks, or passes. The top bidder picks trump, then the other 8 cards are dealt so everyone holds 13, and the bidder leads.
  • Follow the suit led if you can. A trump beats any other suit; otherwise the highest card of the led suit wins.
  • If the bidders take at least their bid, they score exactly their bid, even if they take more (16 for a bid of 13). If not, they lose their bid. The other team must take at least 5 tricks (13 minus the bid when the bid is 9 or more): if it does, it scores +5 and never more; if it falls short, it loses 5.
